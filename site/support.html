Getting started

Add the players on your Yahoo, ESPN, or Fantrax roster on the Roster tab (search any NHL player), then set your league's lineup slots in League rules. PuckIQ never signs in to your league. You make the moves in your fantasy app.

Why is a player's eligibility wrong?

Leagues set positions differently. Open the player and tap the positions your league allows (for example C and LW). The lineup uses exactly what you set.

Starting goalies

The NHL doesn't announce starting goalies before puck drop, so PuckIQ shows how often each goalie has started instead of guessing.

PuckIQ Pro

Pro adds tonight's best lineup, every coach move, the week planner, full pickup rankings, the matchup view, player trends, NHL Edge stats, and up to 5 leagues. It's billed through your Apple ID and renews automatically until you cancel.

  • Cancel or change plans: Manage subscriptions (or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • New phone or reinstall: tap Restore purchases in Settings.
  • Bought PuckIQ before it went free? Pro is included. If it doesn't show, tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Lineup reminders

Turn on reminders in Settings to get one heads-up before your first player's puck drop, only on nights your players play. If none arrive, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → PuckIQ.

Backup and deleting your account

Sign in with Apple in Settings to back up your teams. To delete your account and backup, go to Settings → Backup → Delete account.

Still stuck?

In the app, tap Settings → About → Send feedback. It includes your app version and iPhone model for you. Or email zlce.app@gmail.com with your iPhone model and the app version (Settings → About).
