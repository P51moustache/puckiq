Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026

HomeSupport

The short version

  • PuckIQ works without an account. Your roster and settings stay on your phone unless you turn on backup.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. We never sell your data.
  • Anonymous usage stats help us improve the app, and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • PuckIQ never signs in to your fantasy league and never changes your lineup.

What stays on your device

The players you add, your league's lineup slots and scoring, and your preferences are stored on your phone. Lineup reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS; we don't use push notification servers.

Optional account and backup

If you choose Continue with Apple in Settings, Apple shares a user identifier and an email address with us (you can choose Apple's private relay address). We use them only to back up your teams and restore them on a new phone. The account and backup are stored with Supabase, our backend provider.

Delete your account any time in Settings → Backup → Delete account. That permanently removes the account and its backup. Teams on your phone stay.

Purchases

PuckIQ Pro is sold through Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat processes your subscription status using Apple's purchase receipts and an app user ID: an anonymous ID, or your PuckIQ account ID if you've signed in.

Anonymous usage stats

To learn which features are useful, the app sends usage events to PostHog: screens viewed and features used (for example, opening a player, adding a pickup, sharing a card, viewing the Pro screen), whether you're on Pro, the app version, and your device's operating system. These events are tied to a random ID created when you install the app. They are not linked to your account, email, or roster, and are never used for advertising.

Turn this off in Settings → About → Share anonymous usage stats.

Hockey data

The app loads schedules, scores, and stats from the NHL's public data services, player photos from the NHL's image servers, and news headlines from ESPN's public feed. Like any website, those servers see your device's IP address when the app requests data. We don't send them anything about you.

Service providers

  • Apple: purchases and Sign in with Apple
  • Supabase: optional account and team backup
  • RevenueCat: subscription status
  • PostHog: anonymous usage stats

Retention

Your backup is kept until you delete your account. Anonymous usage events are kept only as long as they're useful for improving the app.

Children

PuckIQ is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can use PuckIQ without an account, turn off usage stats, and delete your account in the app. To ask about or request deletion of any data we hold, email zlce.app@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
