# PuckIQ — loose-prompt explorations

Four independent boards generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Prompts stayed short, retained the current palette, and left layout, type, navigation, imagery, and visual personality to the model. Names below describe the resulting images; they were not prescribed as design directions.

- [04 — Sports editorial](04-sports-editorial.png): Condensed typography, photography, and compact information.
- [05 — Graphic campaign](05-graphic-campaign.png): Large type, diagonal red graphics, monochrome player imagery, and rink roster.
- [06 — Serif editorial](06-serif-editorial.png): Serif headlines, airy layouts, and subtle schedule dots.
- [07 — Personal dashboard](07-personal-dashboard.png): Personalized overview, photographic player detail, and lineup assistant.

These are brainstorming mockups. Generated features, copy, player information, and statistics are illustrative and do not establish existing app behavior.
