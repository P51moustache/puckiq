## Combined direction

Center the app on players and lineup decisions. A player page explains who the player is, recent performance, projected contribution and upcoming schedule fit. The lineup tool brings starter choices, bench alternatives and coaching together. Insights offers personal advice and player discovery.

Keep near-black dark mode, charcoal panels, red highlights, readable compact typography, subtle borders and cinematic hockey art. The final board restores the requested “Add to starters” bottom action on the player page, alongside its schedule assessment.

The proposed navigation and data values are part of the visual exploration. Player statistics, forecasts, ratings, rostered percentages and advice are illustrative. The exact semantics of starter actions, projection models and schedule ratings remain implementation decisions; external fantasy lineup changes are user actions.
