Simpler screens, sharper lineup calls, and an "Open Yahoo" button that works.

• "Open Yahoo" now works every time. It opens the Yahoo app or website, or a browser inside PuckIQ if neither can open.
• The free lineup call never suggests a player whose game has already started, and Insights stops suggesting streamers once every game that night has started.
• Added only part of your team? Tonight reminds you to add the rest, so every call covers your whole roster.
• Tonight, Players, Lineup and Pickups are simpler, with fewer duplicate cards and shorter notes, so your players come first.
• Privacy, Terms and Settings links open reliably too, with an in-app browser as a fallback.

Saved plans stay in PuckIQ. You make the final moves in Yahoo, ESPN or Fantrax.
