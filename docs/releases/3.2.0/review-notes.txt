NEW IN THIS 3.2 BUILD:
Tabs: Home, Players, Lineup, Insights and More. More opens Week, Pickups, League Room and Settings. The gear opens Settings.

Player details: tap a player in Players, Lineup or an insight. Overview includes public NHL stats, recent-performance charts, upcoming games and estimated season outlooks. Owned players can save start/bench choices for a date with Pro.

Lineup: compare Best lineup and Edit my lineup. Choose dates up to 14 days ahead. Apply saves a local PuckIQ plan; it does not change a Yahoo/ESPN/Fantrax team. Edits lock at the day's first puck drop. Missing stats withhold recommendations instead of inventing values.

Insights: tap a recommendation to open its complete reason and affected players. The next action opens Lineup or Pickup finder. Goalie outlooks, projections and likely-rostered estimates are explicitly tentative.

Screenshots show native UI, a labeled sample roster and real NHL-derived stats/estimates, including Pro. Cinematic background art is decorative.

ALSO IN 3.2:
- Roster import: on the setup players step (or Players > scan icon), "Import from screenshots" opens the system photo picker (PHPicker, no photo-library permission) and reads player names on the device with Vision. Nothing is uploaded. "Paste or type names" works without photos.
- Category leagues: setup asks "Points or Categories"; League settings (Players > league rules card) and a one-time question on Tonight can switch it. Tonight then shows category totals.
- Lineup reminders name the swaps to make (Settings > Game-night reminders > "Only when my lineup needs a change").
- Goalie start outlook on Tonight and the Week grid: estimates from public NHL schedules and box scores, never "confirmed".
- "Make these moves in Yahoo" opens the Yahoo, ESPN or Fantrax website or app (or a team page the user pastes in League settings). PuckIQ still never signs in to or changes a fantasy league.

OTHER FEATURES:

League Room: More > League Room > Sign in with Apple. Create a private room or join with its code/link. Names are filtered; reactions use fixed emojis, with no free-text chat. Members can leave; owners can remove teams. The room menu offers Report room (emails support). Opponents and Pickups taken marks populate as members join.

Player alerts: Settings > Player alerts. A push only when a player on the user's roster is scratched (NHL game report) or scores. The push token and player IDs are not linked to an account.

Widgets: Home Screen (small, medium) and Lock Screen. Live Activity: during live NHL games, Tonight > "Follow on Lock Screen". Tonight shows live fantasy points during games and, until noon ET, a recap of the night before.

PuckIQ helps users manage their existing Yahoo/ESPN/Fantrax roster without signing in to or changing those leagues. No betting or real-money contests.

No account needed. On first launch tap "Set up my team", then on the players step tap "Just looking? Try a sample team" to load a full roster (a card offers "Remove sample players" later). Or search any NHL player (e.g. "McDavid").

Public NHL APIs supply schedule/stats. Prior-season fallback is labeled.

PuckIQ is free to download. PuckIQ Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: Season (yearly, 7-day free trial) or Monthly. Open the paywall from Settings > See Pro or any PRO card (e.g. Tonight > Best lineup). Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. People who bought the old paid app keep Pro automatically (checked from the App Store receipt).

Sign in with Apple is optional; it backs up teams and is needed for League Room. Account deletion: Settings > Backup > Delete account (shown when signed in). Feedback: Settings > About > Send feedback.

Sandbox review: Apple’s fixed 2013-08-01 sandbox original-download date is ignored for paid-app migration and the returning-user gift. A new sandbox user remains free and can test the paywall and subscriptions; active purchased entitlements still unlock Pro.
