# Arena Club through the hockey year

Owner direction, September10: make appearance and available activities fit offseason, preseason, regular season and playoffs, with the strongest daily value during the season. Builds on the approved Arena Club identity.

The home tab becomes Home so it remains meaningful when there is no game tonight. The same team palette, jersey type, original skater art, raised buttons and saved game cards stay consistent.

| Phase | Home emphasis | Useful actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offseason | Team clubhouse poster with jersey artwork; no months-old game as the lead story | Build a player watchlist, choose followed teams, revisit the season book and league baseline |
| Preseason | Camp preparation poster; actual exhibition games where available | Watch players, open the next matchup, distinguish exhibitions from regular-season statistics |
| Regular season | Original game-night poster and saved forecast comparisons | Open team matchup, track followed players, save the pregame card and compare its result |
| Playoffs | Game-night poster with playoff identity and a series result strip | Inspect known results in the same series, open each game, save/revisit expectations |

An interactive rink-shaped year guide shows how the same app changes focus. Selecting a guide segment moves its puck and explains that phase; it does not change the actual season or fabricate game data. Reduced-motion users get the same states without movement.

Phase selection uses credible nearby schedule evidence first. Calendar fallback is explicit, uses no hardcoded season year and never turns an absent/stale feed into a claim that hockey is not being played. Upcoming September games do not make July preseason. Current dates use NHL Eastern time. Playoff counts are explicitly the results present in the feed, not a fabricated complete bracket or championship claim.

The mobile app continues to read Supabase only. No new native dependency or production database migration is required. Existing follow/player/league/season-book actions provide current utility; unsigned trades, roster news, confirmed starters and paid alerts are not invented.
