# Music prompt for the PuckIQ App Store preview

Generate an instrumental in Suno Custom mode with Instrumental enabled. The video is 25 seconds. Ask for a 25–30 second cue; if Suno returns a longer take, use the strongest 25 seconds and keep a clean ending.

## Prompt

Premium fantasy hockey app launch instrumental, 128 BPM, dark electronic sports pulse. Immediate hook in the first half-second: tight punchy kick, dry snare, crisp hi-hats, deep controlled synth bass, icy plucked synth motif. Confident, focused, competitive; sleek and modern. Small percussion lifts around 4.5, 9, 14 and 17 seconds for app feature cuts. Build energy through 22 seconds, then a satisfying final hit at 24 seconds with a short clean decay to silence at 25 seconds. Compact 25-second advertising cue. No vocals, chants, spoken words, crowd noise, long intro, epic orchestral swell, or recognizable song references. Clear midrange and restrained bass so the app remains the focus.

## Matching the edit

| Video time | Feature | Music direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4.5 s | Tonight | Hook immediately; steady pulse |
| 4.5–9 s | Best lineup | Small lift |
| 9–14 s | Week planner | Bass and main motif lock in |
| 14–17 s | Matchup | Brief percussion accent |
| 17–22 s | Pickups | Strongest groove |
| 22–25 s | Make every game count | Resolve and leave a clean tail |

Create the track while subscribed to Suno Pro or Premier for commercial usage rights; Suno says free-plan tracks are for noncommercial use. Save the song link and creation date with the export. [Suno commercial usage guidance](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2416769)

Drop the downloaded music in `marketing/input/audio/`. The current original-music preview uses an earlier synthesized soundtrack as a placeholder; it is not a Suno generation. The music-ready preview includes silent AAC audio for a straightforward replacement.
